## 📖 Executive Summary
In modern financial markets, the seamless transition from data analysis to strategy execution is a critical competitive advantage. This terminal is a proprietary full-stack software solution that combines complex financial mathematical models, live market data, and portfolio management into a coherent architecture. 

It provides institutional-grade tools—ranging from stochastic Monte Carlo simulations and Market Making algorithms to deep cointegration screening for Statistical Arbitrage (StatArb).

---

## ✨ Core Features & Modules

The platform is divided into specialized modules covering the entire workflow of a quantitative portfolio manager:

### 1. Strategy Discovery & Statistical Arbitrage (StatArb)
* **Universe Screener:** Automated analysis of entire sectors. The algorithm computes correlation heatmaps and tests hundreds of asset pairs simultaneously for statistical anomalies.
* **Cointegration & Pairs Trading:** Identifies assets with strong mean-reversion behavior (Engle-Granger test), calculates hedge ratios (Beta), and generates Z-Score-based entry/exit signals for market-neutral trades.
* **Lead-Lag Cross-Correlation:** Uncovers causalities and time-delayed relationships to develop predictive trading signals.

### 2. Quantitative Fundamental Analysis
* **Altman Z-Score:** Algorithmic evaluation of current corporate balance sheets to predict bankruptcy risks (Credit Risk), visualizing the output into Safe, Grey, or Distress Zones.
* **Drawdown Risk:** Generates historical "Underwater Charts" to identify maximum drawdown risks beyond standard volatility.

### 3. Analytical Derivatives Pricing (Black-Scholes)
* **Theoretical Pricing:** Real-time calculation of European options with dynamic adjustments for volatility, interest rates, and time to maturity.
* **Risk Sensitivities ("The Greeks"):** Interactive visualization of $\Delta$, $\Gamma$, $\Theta$, $\mathcal{V}$, and $\rho$ to anticipate derivative behavior during market shocks.

### 4. Stochastic Modeling (Monte Carlo)
* Utilizes the renowned **QuantLib** engine to simulate thousands of potential future asset paths.
* Models correlated asset price developments and visualizes future probability corridors to evaluate tail risks.

### 5. Market Maker & Delta Hedging Engine
* **Inventory Skewing:** Dynamic, asymmetric adjustment of bid-ask spreads based on the market maker's current inventory risk.
* **Automated Hedging:** Continuous calculation of the overall portfolio delta, providing actionable instructions to maintain a delta-neutral stance.

### 6. CIO Strategy Board (Kanban)
* An integrated Kanban system backed by a local SQLite database to manage the investment process. 
* Portfolio Managers can document investment theses, allocate capital, and visually monitor the risk budget across the entire fund.

## 🏗️ Technological Architecture

Unlike monolithic legacy systems, this terminal relies on a modern, decoupled architecture:

* **API-First Design (FastAPI):** The mathematical backend (pricing, stochastic calculations) is decoupled into a RESTful API. This ensures a strict separation of computational logic and UI, allowing seamless integration of future clients (e.g., mobile apps, trading bots).
* **Live Data Integration:** Direct connection to global market data providers (via `yfinance`) processing historical prices, returns, and balance sheets in real-time.
* **Local Persistence:** Utilizes a lightweight, integrated SQL database (`SQLite`) to securely store watchlists and strategy planning boards locally.
